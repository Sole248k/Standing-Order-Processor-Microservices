Scenario 1

Successful Transfer

Expected: Source ₱15,000; Destination ₱6,000; Notification delivered.
Scenario 2

Insufficient Funds

Source ₱2,000 (EWB-SAL-1002); Transfer ₱5,000.

Expected: Occurrence fails; balances unchanged; recurring schedule preserved.
Scenario 3

Frozen Account

Source is FROZEN (EWB-SAL-FROZEN); Transfer ₱5,000.

Expected: Rejected payment with reason; no ledger movement.
Scenario 4

Duplicate Request Prevention

Submit same occurrence / idempotency key twice.

Expected: Exactly one debit and one credit; cached result returned.
Scenario 5

Lost Response & Recovery

Payment commits, then simulated timeout happens.

Expected: Execution marked UNRESOLVED; recovery resolves SUCCESS; NO second debit.
Scenario 6

Paused Instruction

Pause standing order before execution.

Expected: Instruction ignored by scheduler; no new payment executed.
Scenario 7

Month-End Schedule (Day 31)

Order scheduled for day 31 of every month.

Expected: In shorter months (Feb 28/29, Apr 30), executes on last day of month.
Scenario 8

Notification Outage & Retry

Payment succeeds while notification service is down.

Expected: Payment commits safely; outbox retries when service recovers.
